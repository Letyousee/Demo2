Our Approach

FastTrack CRO Methodology

The FastTrack process applies the 80/20 principle — identify the 20% of changes that drive 80% of conversion lift. Every recommendation is anchored to published research and directly observable issues on your site.

01

Audit the Buyer Journey

Walk every path from landing to checkout — identifying friction, missing trust signals, and broken experiences at each step.

03

Critical Issue #2

About Us Page Is Invisible
to Google

Fix Immediately — SEO & Trust Risk

What We Found

Your About Us page contains: googlebot: noindex,indexifembedded — telling Google's crawler to exclude the page entirely from search results. For a brand founded in 1972 with a compelling heritage story, your About page is one of your most powerful trust-building and SEO assets. Google currently cannot index it.

🔍
Not Indexed by Google
noindex meta tag active
📉
Zero Brand Search Visibility
"About Sundance Leather" → no result
🏆
54-Year Heritage Story Hidden
Competitors rank; you don't
1
Shift4Shop admin → About Us page → SEO settings tab
2
Remove noindex,indexifembedded → set to index,follow
3
Google Search Console → URL Inspection → Request Indexing
4
Time: 15 minutes. Indexing takes 1–4 weeks; fix is instant.
