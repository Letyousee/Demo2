Our Approach

FastTrack CRO Methodology

The FastTrack CRO process applies the 80/20 principle: identify the 20% of changes that drive 80% of conversion lift. Every recommendation is tied to published data, case studies, and directly observable issues on your site.

01

Audit the Buyer Journey

Walk every path from landing to checkout — homepage, category, product detail, cart, contact — identifying friction, missing signals, and broken trust at each step.

02

Evidence-Backed Fixes Only

Every suggestion is anchored to third-party research (Baymard, Nielsen Norman, Unbounce) and/or real A/B test results from comparable e-commerce sites.

03

Quick Wins First

Prioritize changes that can be live within 1–2 weeks — copy edits, badge placement, trust signals — before tackling structural or code-intensive changes.

Critical Issue #1

Legacy Domain Links Active
on Every Page

Fix Immediately — Revenue Risk

What We Found

The "Why Buy Sundance?" sidebar link in your navigation points to the old Shift4Shop staging domain: sundanceleather-com.3dcartstores.com. This link appears on every page of your site. While it currently redirects, staging domains are unstable — any infrastructure change at Shift4Shop breaks this link sitewide, sending customers to a dead page at the moment they're most ready to buy.

Critical Issue #2

About Us Page Is Invisible
to Google

Fix Immediately — SEO & Trust Risk

What We Found

Your About Us page contains the meta tag: googlebot: noindex,indexifembedded. This tells Google's crawler to exclude the page from search results entirely. For a brand founded in 1972 with a compelling heritage story, your "About Us" is one of your most powerful trust-building and SEO assets — and Google currently cannot index it.

🏆

Competitors rank for brand + "since 1972" terms
1
Open About Us page in Shift4Shop admin → Page SEO settings → Remove or change the robots directive
2
Replace noindex,indexifembedded with index,follow (or remove the tag entirely)
3
Submit page to Google Search Console → URL Inspection → Request Indexing
4
Time investment: 15 minutes. Indexing may take 1–4 weeks but the fix is instant
01
CRO Suggestion

Add a Direct CTA Button
to the Hero Carousel

Quick Win — 1 Day

📊 Case Study / Data

80%

Nielsen Norman Group research shows 80% of user attention is spent above the fold. Hero sections without a clear CTA button lose the majority of visitor intent within seconds of landing.

📍 Current Scenario

Your homepage carousel rotates through product images with no call-to-action button overlay. Visitors who arrive ready to shop see a beautiful image but have no obvious next step — they must independently find the navigation or scroll to "Featured Products."

✅ Recommendation

  • Add a leather-toned CTA button to each carousel slide, e.g. "Shop Moccasins", "Explore Boots"
  • Position button center-bottom of each slide image
  • Use high-contrast button (cognac on dark or white on image)
  • Ensure each slide links directly to its corresponding category page

🏪 Competitor Example

Minnetonka Moccasin and Frye Boots both place "Shop Now" and "Explore Collection" buttons directly on hero images with high-contrast overlays — guiding visitors to category pages within 2 seconds of landing, eliminating the search for next steps.

02
CRO Suggestion

Make the Search Bar Visible
in the Header

Quick Win — 2 Days

📊 Case Study / Data

+9% CVR

LYST (fashion e-commerce) found that making search visible (vs. hidden behind icon) increased search usage by +43% and overall conversion rate by +9%. Baymard Institute: shoppers who use search convert at 2–3x the rate of those who browse.

📍 Current Scenario

Your search function is hidden behind a small magnifying glass icon in the top-right corner. On a site with 12 categories and dozens of SKUs across multiple sizing systems, customers looking for a specific style — e.g. "black moccasin size 10" — cannot find it without discovering the hidden icon first.

✅ Recommendation

  • Replace the icon with a visible search bar in the header, spanning 200–280px
  • Placeholder text: "Search styles, sizes, materials…"
  • On mobile, keep the icon but make the expanded bar full-width on tap
  • Enable autocomplete suggestions if your platform supports it

🏪 Competitor Example

Zappos and Chaco feature prominent center-header search bars on all pages. Zappos specifically cites search as one of their top conversion-driving features — helping customers find the right fit faster and reducing frustration-driven exits.

03
CRO Suggestion

Add Payment Method Logos
to Footer & Checkout

Quick Win — 1 Day

📊 Case Study / Data

6%

Baymard Institute's 2022 checkout study found that 6% of shoppers abandon specifically because they can't see their preferred payment method listed upfront. Payment logos increase checkout confidence before the buyer even clicks "Add to Cart."

📍 Current Scenario

Your footer and product pages display no payment method logos. The only trust badge visible is a small RapidSSL seal in the sidebar — which most customers don't recognize or register. First-time buyers have no visual confirmation that Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal are accepted until they reach checkout — a point many never reach.

✅ Recommendation

  • Add a row of payment logos to the footer: Visa, MC, Amex, Discover, PayPal
  • Add same row beneath "Add to Cart" on product pages
  • Replace the low-recognition RapidSSL badge with a recognizable Norton / McAfee / BBB badge
  • Add a "Secure Checkout" lock icon near the cart CTA

🏪 Competitor Example

Soft Star Shoes and Earth Shoes display Visa/MC/PayPal/Amex logos prominently in their footers and on product pages. This single change is cited as one of the fastest trust-signal wins in e-commerce — free to implement, immediate psychological effect.

04
CRO Suggestion

Surface Your 90-Day Guarantee
Near "Add to Cart"

Quick Win — 2 Days

📊 Case Study / Data

+34%

Copyblogger research: displaying a guarantee or return policy directly adjacent to the purchase button increases conversions by up to +34%. The guarantee must be near the moment of hesitation — not on a separate page a click away.

📍 Current Scenario

Sundance offers an outstanding 90-day satisfaction guarantee with hassle-free exchanges and no return merchandise authorization required. This is a major competitive advantage — but it is buried on the Shipping & Returns page. Product pages show no mention of it. Buyers considering a $150+ leather moccasin see no safety net at their decision moment.

✅ Recommendation

  • Add a 3-icon trust row beneath every "Add to Cart" button:
  • 🛡 90-Day Guarantee | 🔄 Free Exchanges | 🇺🇸 Made in USA
  • Make "90-Day Guarantee" a hyperlink to the Returns page for buyers who want the full detail
  • Optional: add a short inline line — "Not the right fit? We'll swap it. No hassle."

🏪 Competitor Example

Danner Boots and Oboz Footwear both display guarantee icons directly below Add to Cart — "Free Returns," "Lifetime Repair Warranty," "Made in USA." This technique is especially powerful for specialty footwear where sizing uncertainty is the #1 hesitation.

CRO Suggestion

Surface Your 150+ Reviews
on the Homepage

High Impact — 1 Week

📊 Case Study / Data

+72%

Unbounce and Broomberg research: adding customer testimonials and social proof to a landing/home page increases conversions by up to +72%. For first-time visitors, peer reviews are more persuasive than any brand claim you can make about yourself.

📍 Current Scenario

Your site has 150+ product reviews averaging 4.97 stars — a near-perfect score that is extraordinary for handmade goods. These reviews are only visible on individual product pages after a visitor navigates there. Your homepage — the first impression for most visitors — shows no reviews, no stars, and no social proof of any kind.

✅ Recommendation

  • Add a "What Customers Are Saying" section between hero and featured products
  • Display 3–4 rotating review quotes with ★★★★★ stars, reviewer name, and product
  • Include an aggregate badge: "4.97★ from 150+ verified customers"
  • Source from your existing product reviews — no new content needed

🏪 Competitor Example

Minnetonka prominently displays "4.8 stars, 12,000+ reviews" in their homepage header. Birkenstock uses a scrolling review carousel above the fold. Both brands treat their review count as a headline feature — Sundance's 4.97 average is actually better than most competitors and deserves the same prominent placement.

CRO Suggestion

Add a Mobile Sticky
"Add to Cart" Bar

High Impact — 1 Week

📊 Case Study / Data

+252.9%

Convertica case study: adding a sticky "Add to Cart" bar on mobile product pages increased order completions by +252.9%. Mobile shoppers frequently scroll past the main ATC button and lose access to it — a sticky bar eliminates this friction permanently.

📍 Current Scenario

On mobile, your "Add to Cart" button is positioned mid-page beneath the product images and description. As customers scroll to read sizing information, product details, or reviews, the purchase button disappears above the screen. To buy, they must scroll back up — a friction point that triggers a significant percentage of mobile exits.

✅ Recommendation

  • Add a fixed bottom bar on mobile that appears when the main ATC button scrolls out of view
  • Bar content: product name (truncated) + price + "Add to Cart" button
  • Disappears when user scrolls back to the main button (no visual duplication)
  • Shift4Shop supports custom HTML/CSS in templates — implementable without a plugin

🏪 Competitor Example

Thursday Boot Co. and Tecovas both use mobile sticky purchase bars on all product pages. Thursday Boot attributes their mobile conversion rate (one of the highest in direct-to-consumer footwear) in part to eliminating scroll-related purchase friction on smartphones.

CRO Suggestion

Add "Best Sellers" Section
with Badges on Products

High Impact — 3–5 Days

📊 Case Study / Data

80%

ROIHunter data: the top 10% of SKUs drive 80% of e-commerce revenue. Explicitly labeling best-sellers reduces decision paralysis, accelerates purchase decisions, and steers buyers toward products most likely to satisfy — reducing returns as a bonus.

📍 Current Scenario

Your homepage shows a "Featured Products" section of 10 items with no differentiation — no badges, no "popular," no "top-rated" indicators. First-time visitors facing 12 product categories and dozens of SKUs have no guidance on where to start. Choice paralysis leads to inaction. Without a "bestseller" signal, customers treat all items as equally uncertain.

✅ Recommendation

  • Rename the homepage section from "Featured Products" to "Best Sellers"
  • Add a "Best Seller" badge (small ribbon/overlay) to your top 5 products by revenue
  • Add a "Customer Favorite" badge to items with 50+ reviews
  • Consider adding a dedicated "Best Sellers" category page in the navigation

🏪 Competitor Example

Manitobah Mukluks uses "Best Seller" ribbons and "#1 Most Loved" badges on their top products — guiding first-time visitors toward proven choices. Blundstone features a "Most Popular" section prominently on their homepage, reducing the decision complexity for new visitors unfamiliar with their range.

CRO Suggestion

Add a Timed Email Capture
Popup (60-Second Delay)

High Impact — 3 Days

📊 Case Study / Data

+72%

Broomberg/Unbounce research: a well-timed email popup (delay 45–90 seconds) increases email list signups by +72%. The key is timing — immediate popups annoy visitors before they've seen your value; a 60-second delay catches engaged visitors at peak interest.

📍 Current Scenario

Sundance Leather currently has no email capture popup. The only email collection point is a small newsletter signup at the very bottom of the footer — a location most visitors never reach. Visitors who browse, don't convert on this visit, and leave are lost forever with no way to re-engage them, even if they were genuinely interested.

✅ Recommendation

  • Add a lightbox popup triggered 60 seconds after page load (or at 50% scroll depth)
  • Offer an incentive: "Get 10% off your first order + artisan stories from our workshop"
  • One field only: email address. Minimize friction.
  • Suppress for return visitors (cookie-based) and on checkout pages

🏪 Competitor Example

Nashoba Valley Winery (craft/artisan brand) saw a +40% increase in email signups using a 45-second delayed popup offering "insider stories" — no discount. For an artisan brand like Sundance with a compelling heritage story, the offer doesn't need to be a discount; exclusive content or "moccasin care tips" can drive equal signup rates.

CRO Suggestion

Rewrite Button Copy:
"Select Options" → "Shop Now"

Quick Win — 1 Day

📊 Case Study / Data

+111%

PartnerStack A/B test: changing a CTA from a system default label to a benefit-oriented, action-clear label increased click-through rate by +111%. Button copy is one of the highest-ROI changes in CRO — it costs nothing to change text.

📍 Current Scenario

Your category pages display "Select Options" as the CTA on all product cards. Your contact form's submit button reads "Submit Request". Your empty cart page shows a lone "Submit" button with no context. These are all system-default labels — cold, mechanical, and zero-benefit. They tell customers what to do to the system, not what they'll get.

✅ Recommendation

  • Category cards: "Select Options" → "Shop Now" or "Choose Your Size"
  • Contact form: "Submit Request" → "Send My Question"
  • Empty cart: Remove the orphaned "Submit" button or replace with "Start Shopping →"
  • These changes require only template text edits — no code changes needed

🏪 Competitor Example

Cariuma (sustainable footwear) uses "Find My Size" and "Add to My Bag" instead of "Select Options" and "Add to Cart" — transforming functional labels into ownership language. Their conversion rate is consistently cited as above industry average, partly attributed to this personalization of purchase language throughout the site.

CRO Suggestion

Show Shipping Speed
Near "Add to Cart"

Quick Win — 1 Day

📊 Case Study / Data

47%

Baymard Institute: 47% of shoppers check shipping speed before completing a purchase. Displaying shipping timing near the Add to Cart button eliminates one of the most common pre-checkout hesitations — especially for gift purchases or time-sensitive needs.

📍 Current Scenario

Shipping information is entirely absent from product pages. Customers must navigate to a separate "Shipping Info" page to discover when their order will arrive. For handmade goods — where buyers often expect long lead times — the lack of visible shipping speed creates unnecessary hesitation. Many buyers assume "handcrafted = weeks of wait" when in reality most orders ship in 1–2 business days.

✅ Recommendation

  • Add a single line beneath "Add to Cart": "🚚 Ships in 1–2 business days via USPS"
  • For expedited option awareness: "Need it fast? UPS 2-Day available at checkout"
  • During peak seasons, add: "Order by Dec 18 for Christmas delivery"
  • This line belongs in the same trust-signal row as the guarantee icons (Suggestion #4)

🏪 Competitor Example

L.L. Bean displays "Free Shipping on orders $50+" and estimated delivery dates directly on every product page. Duluth Trading Co. shows "Ships in 1–2 days" beneath their ATC button — a practice that has been A/B tested to increase purchase confidence for customers evaluating time-sensitive buying decisions.
